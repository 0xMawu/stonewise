About Stonewise

Who We Are

Built on a Foundation of Integrity

Stonewise Construction was founded on a single belief: that every client deserves a building partner who is as invested in the outcome as they are. Since our founding, we have grown from a small team of dedicated craftsmen into one of the region's most trusted full-service construction firms.

We operate across residential, commercial, condominium, lodge, and infrastructure sectors — bringing the same rigour, transparency, and passion to a single-family home as we do to a 12-storey high-rise. Our team of over 180 professionals includes civil engineers, project managers, site supervisors, quantity surveyors, and specialist trades, all unified by our commitment to quality.

Based in Accra, Ghana, we have delivered projects across the Greater Accra, Ashanti, Western, and Eastern regions, with a growing pipeline of international commissions.

Our Core Values

01

Uncompromising Quality

Every beam, every finish, every detail is held to the highest standard. We never cut corners — we believe the buildings we create will outlast us, so we build accordingly.

02

Radical Transparency

From the first quotation to final handover, we communicate openly. No hidden costs, no surprises — just honest, clear collaboration at every stage of the project.

03

Respect for Time

Deadlines are commitments, not targets. Our disciplined programme management and proactive risk mitigation ensure we deliver on schedule — every time.

04

People First

We invest in our team, our clients, and our communities. Safe working conditions, fair wages, and genuine care for the people behind every project are non-negotiable.

05

Safety Above All

Our zero-harm safety culture permeates every site, every shift. Rigorous training, strict protocols, and continuous improvement keep our people and sites protected.

06

Community Legacy

We build more than structures — we build communities. Sustainable practices, local sourcing, and community investment are central to how we operate and grow.

Our Journey

A Decade and a Half of Building Excellence

2009

Foundation

Stonewise Construction is established by a small team of engineers and craftsmen in Accra with a commitment to delivering quality residential builds that clients could trust. The first project — a three-bedroom residence in Tema — is completed ahead of schedule.

2012

Commercial Expansion

Stonewise wins its first major commercial contract — a 6-storey office building in the Central Business District. The successful delivery of this project establishes the firm's reputation in the commercial sector and drives rapid team growth to 40 staff.

2015

Condominium Division

A dedicated condominium and high-rise division is formed. Stonewise begins work on its first multi-storey residential tower — an 8-floor, 64-unit development in East Legon that sells out before completion.

2018

Hospitality & Lodges

Stonewise diversifies into lodge and hospitality construction, completing Bayside Lodge on the Volta Lake — its first destination hospitality project. The project wins regional recognition for architectural sensitivity and build quality.

2021

Infrastructure & Public Works

Stonewise is awarded its first infrastructure contract: the Crossroads Highway interchange project in Kasoa. This milestone establishes the firm as a full-spectrum contractor capable of delivering complex civil engineering works alongside premium builds.

2024

Granite Heights & Beyond

Completion of Granite Heights — the firm's most ambitious project to date: a 12-storey, 120-unit luxury condominium tower in East Legon valued at GH₵ 48 million. Stonewise celebrates 15 years in business with over 200 completed projects and a team of 180+ professionals.
