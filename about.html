Our Story

Why We Started Stonewise

The Problem We Exist to Solve

The Diaspora Deserves Better

Stonewise Construction was born from a simple, urgent truth: millions of Ghanaians living abroad want to build or invest in property back home — but far too many have been burned by dishonest contractors, mismanaged funds, or builds that never reached completion.

We founded Stonewise specifically to bridge that trust gap. Our entire operating model is designed around the reality that our clients are in London, Toronto, Amsterdam, Houston, or Sydney — not on-site in Accra. We bring the accountability, the communication systems, and the on-the-ground expertise that makes building from abroad not just possible, but genuinely reassuring.

Based in Accra, with a team that understands both the Ghanaian construction landscape and the concerns of the diaspora, we serve as the trusted eyes, hands, and advocates our clients need — every step of the way.

Our Core Values

01

Radical Honesty

We tell you what is happening on your site — even when it's not good news. No cover-ups, no sugar-coating. You deserve the truth, and you'll always get it from us.

02

Eyes on Your Site, Every Day

Your dedicated site team shows up whether you're watching or not. We operate as if you're standing beside us — because that is the standard our diaspora clients deserve.

03

Your Timeline is Sacred

We know you've made plans around your build — flights home, family expectations, financial commitments. We treat your timeline as a promise, not a guideline.

06

Our Journey

How Stonewise Grew Into Ghana's Diaspora Builder

2022

Founded with a Purpose

Stonewise Construction is established in Accra with one core mission: to give Ghanaians abroad a contractor they can truly trust. Our first client — a nurse based in Manchester — receives the keys to her family home in Tema, built entirely while she managed the process remotely via weekly video calls.

2023

The Diaspora Reporting System Launches

We formalise our proprietary client reporting system — weekly structured updates with site photos, video walkthroughs, spend breakdowns, and milestone certifications. Clients across the UK, US, Canada, and Germany begin building with confidence they'd never had before.

2023

First Commercial Project

A Ghanaian-American investor commissions us for a rental commercial complex in Airport Residential, Accra. Managed entirely remotely, it is delivered on time and becomes a flagship example of what trust-based diaspora construction looks like.

2024

Granite Heights — Our Flagship

Completion of Granite Heights — a 12-storey, 120-unit luxury condominium tower in East Legon, valued at GH₵ 48 million. Several units were purchased by diaspora investors who never visited during construction, relying entirely on our reporting system for peace of mind.

2025

Growing the Team

With a growing pipeline of diaspora commissions across Greater Accra, Ashanti, and the Eastern Region, we expand our team to 50+ professionals — including dedicated Diaspora Liaison Officers who speak the language of our clients' concerns and communicate across time zones.
