Status Completed 2024

Project Overview

A New Standard in Vertical Living

Granite Heights is a 12-storey luxury condominium development comprising 120 residential units across 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, and penthouse configurations. The project was conceived to redefine high-rise living in East Legon — combining premium international finishes with contextually responsive architecture.

Construction encompassed full deep-pile foundation works, reinforced concrete structural frame, high-specification MEP installations, and bespoke interior finishing packages. The building features a rooftop infinity pool, commercial ground floor, underground parking for 200 vehicles, and 24-hour security infrastructure.
