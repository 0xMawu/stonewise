What We Offer

Our Services

End-to-End Construction

Complete Construction Solutions

Stonewise is a full-service contractor delivering across six core disciplines. From concept and planning through to handover and aftercare, our integrated teams ensure seamless delivery of every project, regardless of scale or complexity.

01
Residential Construction

Homes Built Around You

We build bespoke family residences, gated community homes, duplexes, townhouses, and multi-unit residential developments. Every home is constructed to your exact specifications, with our team managing design coordination, materials procurement, and site execution under one roof.

From a compact 3-bedroom dwelling to a sprawling estate, we apply the same level of precision and care — ensuring structural integrity, premium finishes, and a smooth build experience from ground-breaking to key handover.

  • Custom single-family homes
  • Duplexes & townhouses
  • Gated community developments
  • Multi-unit residential estates
  • Foundation & structural works
  • Interior fit-out & finishing
Discuss a Residential Project
02
Commercial & Retail

Commercial Spaces Built for Performance

Our commercial construction capability spans office towers, retail centres, mixed-use developments, and institutional facilities. We understand that commercial buildings must perform for their occupants and investors over decades — so we build with longevity, functionality, and operational efficiency front of mind.

Our commercial projects are delivered with rigorous programme management and transparent reporting, ensuring stakeholders are informed at every stage and budgets are meticulously controlled.

  • Office buildings & towers
  • Retail & shopping centres
  • Mixed-use developments
  • Banks & institutional buildings
  • Warehousing & industrial
  • Medical & educational facilities
Start a Commercial Project
03
Condominiums & Estates

Luxury High-Rise Living, Delivered

Our condominium division specialises in mid-rise and high-rise residential tower construction — from ground investigation and deep-pile foundation engineering through to premium fit-out and landscaping. We understand the specific demands of vertical residential development and have refined our delivery processes accordingly.

Notable completed projects include Granite Heights (120 units, 12 floors, East Legon) and Skyline Condominiums (96 units, 15 floors, Airport Residential). Both projects were delivered on programme and to the highest specification.

  • High-rise structural engineering
  • Deep-pile foundations
  • MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing)
  • Premium unit fit-out
  • Rooftop amenities & pools
  • Underground parking structures
Discuss a Condominium Project
04
Lodges & Hospitality

Spaces Crafted for Atmosphere & Experience

Hospitality builds require a unique blend of structural integrity, atmospheric design, and operational precision. Stonewise has delivered boutique lodges, lakeside retreats, restaurants, lounges, and bar complexes across Ghana — each one crafted to create an unforgettable experience for its guests.

Our hospitality projects are built with the operator in mind: practical back-of-house functionality, durable materials that stand up to intensive use, and guest-facing spaces that leave lasting impressions.

  • Boutique lodges & retreats
  • Restaurants & dining spaces
  • Bars & entertainment lounges
  • Hotel & resort construction
  • Outdoor & pool structures
  • Hospitality fit-out & interiors
Enquire About Hospitality Builds
05
Renovation & Fit-out

Breathing New Life Into Existing Structures

Our renovation and refurbishment team works on occupied and vacant properties, delivering transformative upgrades with minimal disruption. Whether you need a full structural overhaul, a fresh interior fit-out, or targeted repairs, we bring the same professionalism and quality to renovation works as we do to new builds.

All renovation projects begin with a thorough condition survey and detailed scope of works, giving clients full visibility of what is required before a single nail is struck.

  • Full structural renovation
  • Interior fit-out & refurbishment
  • Façade restoration
  • Office & commercial fit-out
  • Plumbing & electrical upgrades
  • Roofing & waterproofing
Get a Renovation Quote
06
Project Management

Your Project, Expertly Managed

For clients who already have construction teams in place, Stonewise offers standalone project management services — providing experienced oversight, programme control, cost management, and quality assurance across any construction project.

Our project managers are trained in PRINCE2 and PMI methodologies, with proven track records across multi-million-cedi developments. We integrate seamlessly with existing teams and provide full transparency through regular reporting, site visits, and stakeholder updates.

  • Programme planning & scheduling
  • Cost management & reporting
  • Quality assurance & inspection
  • Contractor coordination
  • Risk management
  • Handover & closeout

How It Works

Our Delivery Process

Every Stonewise project follows our proven five-stage process — structured to deliver certainty, quality, and satisfaction at every step.

01
Consultation

We meet to understand your vision, budget, and timeline. No obligation, no cost.

02
Assessment & Scope

Our team surveys the site, reviews plans, and prepares a detailed scope of works.

03
Proposal & Contract

We present a transparent, itemised quotation. Once agreed, contracts are signed.

04
Construction

A dedicated site team executes the works with weekly updates and quality checks.

05
Handover

Final inspection, snagging, key handover, and 12-month defects liability period.
