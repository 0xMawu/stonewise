What We Offer

How We Build for You

Designed for the Diaspora

Everything You Need to Build in Ghana — From Wherever You Are

Every service we offer is designed with one client in mind: you — a Ghanaian or African abroad who wants to build back home but can't be there in person. From first consultation to key handover, we manage everything so you don't have to.

01
Family Homes & Residences

The Home You've Been Planning for Years

Whether it's your parents' retirement home in Kumasi, the house you plan to return to one day, or a rental property for extended family — we build it to your exact specification, with you in full control from abroad.

We coordinate design, procurement, permits, and construction from a single point of accountability. You choose your finishes, approve key decisions on video call, and receive weekly photo updates. No need to trust a relative to "check on things" — we are your eyes, ears, and advocates on the ground.

  • Custom family homes
  • Duplexes & townhouses
  • Gated community builds
  • Multi-unit residences
  • Full structural works
  • Interior fit-out & finishing
Discuss Your Home
02
Rental & Commercial Properties

Build an Asset That Works While You're Away

Many of our diaspora clients build commercial or rental properties in Ghana as income-generating investments — office spaces, retail units, apartments, and mixed-use complexes that earn cedi revenue while they remain abroad.

We build with the long-term investor in mind: durable materials, energy-efficient systems, and practical layouts that attract reliable tenants and require minimal maintenance. We can also connect you with property management services post-handover so your asset continues earning without your daily involvement.

  • Office buildings & towers
  • Retail & rental complexes
  • Mixed-use developments
  • Apartment blocks
  • Warehousing & storage
  • Institutional facilities
Discuss Your Investment
03
Condominiums & Estates

Premium Multi-Unit Developments in Ghana's Best Locations

Ghana's condominium market is growing rapidly, and diaspora investors are increasingly commissioning multi-unit developments in East Legon, Airport Residential, Cantonments, and other premium Accra locations as high-yield assets.

Our condominium division has delivered projects from 8 to 120 units — managing everything from ground investigation and pile foundation engineering to premium fit-out and rooftop amenities. Several of these projects were managed entirely remotely by diaspora clients who never set foot on the site during construction.

  • High-rise structural engineering
  • Deep-pile foundations
  • MEP installations
  • Premium unit fit-out
  • Rooftop amenities & pools
  • Underground parking
Discuss a Condo Project
04
Lodges & Hospitality

Ghana's Tourism Boom — Your Opportunity

Ghana's tourism and hospitality sector is one of the fastest-growing investment opportunities in West Africa. Diaspora investors are building boutique lodges, eco-retreats, restaurants, and bar complexes in scenic locations across the Volta Region, Eastern Region, and coastal areas — generating significant short-let and tourist income.

We design and build hospitality properties with both atmosphere and operational functionality in mind — spaces that guests love and that hold up to intensive daily use. Our team understands the practical requirements of hospitality builds: kitchen extraction, guest safety, durable outdoor structures, and systems that run reliably with minimal maintenance.

  • Boutique lodges & eco-retreats
  • Restaurants & dining spaces
  • Bars & entertainment venues
  • Guesthouses & short-let properties
  • Outdoor & pool structures
  • Hospitality interiors & fit-out
Discuss a Hospitality Build
05
Renovation & Property Upgrades

Transform What Already Exists Back Home

Many diaspora clients have inherited family properties in Ghana that need significant renovation, or own older buildings that have deteriorated while they've been away. Stonewise specialises in transforming these properties — bringing them up to a modern standard that reflects your ambitions and adds real value.

We conduct a thorough condition survey before any work begins, giving you a full picture of what's needed and at what cost. All renovation projects are subject to the same weekly reporting and milestone payment system as our new builds — you stay informed and in control throughout.

  • Full structural renovation
  • Interior refurbishment
  • Façade restoration
  • Roofing & waterproofing
  • Plumbing & electrical upgrades
  • Extensions & additions
Get a Renovation Quote
06
Remote Project Management

Already Have a Contractor? Let Us Watch Over Them

Perhaps you already have a contractor in Ghana — a family referral or a local firm — but you need a trusted independent party to supervise the quality, control the spend, and report back to you honestly. This is our Remote Project Management service, designed specifically for diaspora clients who want a professional second pair of eyes on an existing build.

We provide site inspections, quality checks, cost auditing, weekly reporting, and independent milestone verification. We don't replace your contractor — we hold them accountable on your behalf. Many clients have used this service after a previous build went wrong, to ensure it doesn't happen again.

  • Independent site inspections
  • Quality & compliance checks
  • Cost auditing & spend reporting
  • Contractor performance monitoring
  • Weekly client reporting
  • Milestone verification

How It Works

From Your First Message to Key Handover

Every Stonewise project follows our proven five-stage diaspora process — structured to give you control, certainty, and peace of mind from wherever you are.

01
Video Consultation

We meet on a video call at your convenience — UK, US, Canada, Europe. Free, no obligation.

02
Site Assessment

We visit your land in Ghana, verify title, and prepare a detailed scope of works and itemised quote.

03
Digital Contract

We sign electronically. Your milestone payment schedule, reporting cadence, and rights are all agreed in writing.

04
Build with Full Reporting

Your dedicated team builds while you receive weekly photos, videos, and spend breakdowns. Payments release milestone by milestone.

05
Handover

Final inspection, snagging, key handover, and 12-month defects liability. Come home to something you'll be proud of.
