Getting Started
How do I start a project with Stonewise?

The easiest first step is to get in touch via our contact page or call us directly. We'll arrange an initial consultation — at no cost — where we discuss your vision, site conditions, budget range, and timeline. Following that meeting, we'll prepare a detailed scope of works and quotation, typically within 5–7 working days.

Do you handle the permit and planning process?

Yes. Stonewise can manage the full permitting and regulatory approval process on your behalf, including building permits, environmental assessments, and utility connections. Our in-house team has deep familiarity with local authority requirements in Accra and across the regions in which we operate, which helps to streamline approvals and avoid delays.

Do you work with clients who already have an architect or design team?

Absolutely. We regularly work alongside client-appointed architects, interior designers, and consultants. Our role in these arrangements is to serve as the construction contractor, bringing the design to life with precision. We're experienced at operating within multi-consultant teams and integrate smoothly with any design-side professionals you've engaged.

What project sizes do you work on?

We work on projects ranging from individual residential homes to large-scale condominium towers and commercial complexes. Our minimum project value is generally GH₵ 500,000 for construction contracts, though we assess each enquiry on its merits. For renovation and fit-out works, smaller scopes may be accommodated — please contact us to discuss.

Costs & Payments
How is pricing structured?

All our quotations are fully itemised — broken down by trade, materials, labour, and preliminaries. We do not quote lump-sum prices with hidden contingencies. This transparency means you always know exactly what you are paying for and what you receive. Quotations are fixed-price contracts unless you request changes to the agreed scope.

What are your payment terms?

Standard payment terms follow a milestone-based schedule tied to project progress. A typical residential project might be structured as: 20% on contract signing, then progressive payments at agreed construction milestones (e.g. foundation completion, roof completion, plastering, finishes), with a final retention sum released at handover following a satisfactory inspection. Exact terms are negotiated and agreed in the contract.

What happens if costs change during construction?

Any change to the agreed scope — whether client-initiated or due to unforeseen site conditions — is handled through a formal Change Order process. We provide a written costing for the variation before any additional works begin, giving you the opportunity to approve or decline. You will never receive a surprise invoice. Only signed, approved Change Orders result in cost adjustments to the contract sum.

Timelines & Scheduling
How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary significantly by project type and complexity. As a general guide: a standard 3–5 bedroom residential home typically takes 10–18 months from ground-breaking to handover; a mid-rise condominium (6–12 floors) may take 24–36 months; a commercial office building of equivalent scale runs 18–30 months. We will provide a detailed programme — broken down by phase and milestone — as part of our proposal.

What if there are delays?

Our programme management is designed to identify and mitigate risks before they cause delays. We maintain float in our programmes and proactively resource-level to keep on track. In the event of a genuine delay caused by Stonewise, we will notify you immediately, explain the cause, and provide a recovery programme with a revised completion date. Delays caused by client-side decisions, weather, or other force majeure events are handled transparently through our change management process.

Can construction begin before all design details are finalised?

For certain project types, yes. It is possible to commence substructure works (foundations, piling) while superstructure and fit-out designs are still being finalised. This approach — known as "fast-track" construction — can save time but requires careful management to avoid costly late changes. We will advise whether fast-track is appropriate for your specific project during the scoping phase.

Quality & Standards
What quality standards do you work to?

All Stonewise projects are constructed in compliance with the Ghana Building Code, relevant British Standards, and — where specified — international codes such as ACI (American Concrete Institute) standards. We are ISO 9001:2015 certified for Quality Management Systems, meaning our internal quality processes are independently audited and continuously improved. Every project has a dedicated Quality Manager responsible for inspection records, material testing, and compliance documentation.

Do you use locally sourced or imported materials?

We use both. Our default is to specify the best-quality material for each application — whether that is locally manufactured or imported. We have established relationships with leading local suppliers for structural materials, and with international suppliers for specialist finishes, façade systems, and MEP equipment. For premium projects, we can source materials from Europe, Asia, or North America based on your specification and budget.

Can I visit the site during construction?

Yes, absolutely. We encourage client site visits and will arrange regular walk-arounds with your dedicated project manager. For safety reasons, all visitors are required to wear appropriate PPE on site (we provide hard hats, high-visibility vests, and safety footwear). We do ask that visits are pre-arranged rather than unannounced, to ensure the right personnel are available to accompany you.

Communication & Updates
How will I be kept informed of progress?

Every client is assigned a dedicated Client Liaison Officer who serves as your primary point of contact throughout the project. You will receive a weekly written progress report covering work completed, upcoming activities, any issues, and programme status. Milestone completion is documented with photographs. For larger projects, we hold monthly formal progress meetings — in person or via video call — at which programme, cost, and quality are reviewed in detail.

What if I need to make changes after construction has started?

Changes are a natural part of any construction project. Simply notify your Client Liaison Officer, who will arrange for the proposed change to be costed and programmed. You will receive a formal Change Order with the cost and time implications before any work proceeds. We never implement changes without written client approval, protecting both parties from misunderstandings.

Aftercare & Warranty
What warranty do you provide?

All Stonewise projects are covered by a 12-month Defects Liability Period from the date of practical completion. During this period, any defects that arise from workmanship or materials supplied by Stonewise will be rectified at no charge to the client. Structural elements carry a longer warranty period of 10 years. Individual product warranties from manufacturers (roofing, glazing, MEP equipment, etc.) are passed directly to the client at handover.

Do you offer maintenance services after handover?

Yes. We offer optional Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM) contracts for completed projects, covering regular inspection and maintenance of MEP systems, facade elements, and common areas. This is particularly valuable for condominium and commercial developments where ongoing asset management is important. Please contact us for details of our maintenance packages.

What documentation do I receive at handover?

At project handover you will receive a comprehensive O&M (Operations & Maintenance) Manual covering all building systems; as-built drawings and structural calculations; manufacturer warranties and maintenance schedules for all major equipment; materials specifications and finish schedules; inspection and test records; and any statutory certificates (e.g. electrical inspection certificate, building completion certificate). Everything is provided in both print and digital format.
