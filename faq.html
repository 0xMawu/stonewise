Getting Started from Abroad
Can I really manage a build in Ghana without being there?

Yes — and this is exactly what we were built to make possible. The vast majority of our clients are based abroad and never set foot on-site during construction. Our structured weekly reporting system — photos, videos, written updates, and spend breakdowns — means you always know what is happening with your project, regardless of your timezone. Many clients tell us they feel more informed about their Ghana build than about local projects they can physically visit.

I don't have land yet — can you still help me?

Yes. We can assist with land search, site identification, and due diligence — including checking title deeds and confirming land is free from encumbrances or disputes. We strongly recommend letting us verify any land before purchase, as land fraud is unfortunately common and the consequences can be devastating. Think of us as your eyes on the ground before a single cedi is committed.

Do I need to come to Ghana to get started?

Not at all. We can conduct the initial consultation by video call, share design options and quotations digitally, handle contracts electronically, and begin the permitting process on your behalf. Many clients sign up, commission, and begin their build without an initial visit — only arriving in Ghana for the key handover. We accommodate UK, US, Canadian, European, and Australian time zones for all scheduled calls.

Do you handle permits and planning approvals?

Yes. We manage the full permitting process on your behalf — building permits, environmental assessments, utility connections, and any required authority approvals. Our team has deep familiarity with local authority processes across Accra and the major regions. This is a critical service for diaspora clients who cannot easily travel to submit paperwork in person.

Trust & Accountability
How do I know my money is being spent on my project?

We use a milestone-based payment system. You never release funds for work that hasn't happened. Each payment is triggered only after a defined, photographed, and verified construction milestone — foundation completion, structural frame, roofing, plastering, finishes, and so on. We provide a full cost breakdown at each stage, so you can see exactly what was spent and on what. No lump sums, no advance payments for unverified work.

What stops you from cutting corners when I'm not there?

Our entire business reputation is built on diaspora clients — we have no incentive to cut corners and every incentive to do the opposite. Practically, every milestone is independently verified before your next payment is released. We also use third-party structural inspections at key stages, and all our builds carry a 12-month defects liability period and 10-year structural warranty. We are accountable not just morally, but contractually and financially.

Can I appoint someone in Ghana to represent me?

Absolutely. Many clients appoint a trusted family member or friend in Ghana as their local representative. We welcome this and work closely with your representative, keeping them updated and involving them in site visits. However, even without a local representative, our own Diaspora Liaison Officers serve as your on-the-ground advocates — so you are never relying solely on goodwill.

What if something goes seriously wrong during the build?

We notify you immediately — we do not hide problems. If an issue arises from our workmanship or materials, we fix it at no additional cost to you. If an issue arises from unforeseen site conditions or your decisions, we present a transparent Change Order with the cost and time implications before proceeding. Everything is documented in writing. We also carry professional indemnity insurance and public liability insurance, providing financial protection in the event of serious incidents.

Costs & Payments
How is pricing structured? Can I pay in foreign currency?

All quotations are fully itemised — broken down by trade, materials, labour, and preliminaries. We can quote and receive payments in GHS (Ghana Cedi), GBP, USD, EUR, or CAD depending on your preference and your bank's capabilities. We will never quote a vague lump sum — you will always know exactly what each line item costs. Prices are fixed in the agreed currency unless scope changes are requested.

What are your payment terms?

Standard payment terms follow a milestone-based schedule. A typical residential project: 20% on contract signing, then progressive payments tied to verified construction stages (foundation, structure, roofing, plastering, finishes), with a final retention sum released at satisfactory handover. You release each payment only after receiving photographic and written confirmation that the corresponding milestone has been completed. Exact terms are agreed in writing before construction begins.

What happens if costs change during construction?

Any change to the agreed scope — whether you request it or it arises from unforeseen site conditions — triggers a formal Change Order process. We provide a written costing before any additional work begins. You approve or decline in writing. You will never receive a surprise invoice. Cost certainty is one of our core commitments to diaspora clients, because we understand that managing finances from abroad requires no nasty surprises.

Timelines & Scheduling
How long will my build take?

Timelines depend on project type and complexity. A standard 3–5 bedroom family home typically takes 10–18 months from ground-breaking to handover. A mid-rise condominium (6–12 floors) may take 24–36 months. A commercial building of equivalent scale runs 18–30 months. We will provide a detailed programme — broken down by phase and milestone — as part of your proposal. We also provide a realistic build calendar so you can plan your visit for the handover.

What if there are delays?

We will tell you immediately — not at the end when it's too late to adjust your plans. We manage programme risk proactively, maintaining schedule float and resourcing plans to absorb minor disruptions. If a genuine delay occurs that is our responsibility, we provide a recovery programme with a revised completion date in writing. We never ask you to simply "be patient" — we give you facts, causes, and a recovery plan.

Updates & Communication
How often will I hear from you, and through what channels?

Every client receives a weekly site report — a structured document including photos, video walkthroughs, a summary of work completed, upcoming activities, current spend vs. budget, and any issues. Your dedicated Diaspora Liaison Officer is reachable via WhatsApp for day-to-day queries, and we schedule monthly video calls for deeper reviews. We adapt our communication hours to your timezone — whether you're in New York, London, or Dubai.

Can I request extra site visits or video calls?

Yes, at any time. If you want a live video walk-around of your site, your liaison can arrange this with 24 hours' notice. If you want a special call with our structural engineer or site supervisor, we arrange it. We understand that when you can't be there, you need access — and we make that access easy and responsive.

What if I want to make changes after the build has started?

Simply message your Diaspora Liaison Officer. We will assess the change, provide the cost and time impact in writing, and wait for your written approval before proceeding. Changes are a normal part of any build — what matters is handling them transparently. We never implement changes without your written approval, protecting you from both financial surprises and misunderstandings.

Aftercare & Warranty
What warranty do you provide?

All Stonewise projects are covered by a 12-month Defects Liability Period from handover. Any defects from our workmanship or materials during this period are rectified at no charge. Structural elements carry a 10-year warranty. Individual product warranties from manufacturers are passed directly to you at handover. This is particularly important for diaspora clients who may not move in immediately — your building is protected even while it sits empty.

If I'm not moving in right away, can you manage the property?

Yes. Many of our diaspora clients build with the intention of returning in the future, or using the property as a rental asset in the meantime. We offer optional post-handover property management services — covering routine inspections, maintenance scheduling, and rental tenant coordination. Contact us for details on our property management packages.

What documentation will I receive at handover?

At handover you will receive a comprehensive O&M (Operations & Maintenance) Manual; as-built drawings and structural calculations; manufacturer warranties and maintenance schedules; materials specifications; inspection and test records; statutory certificates (electrical inspection certificate, building completion certificate); and a full photo and video archive of the entire build process. Everything is provided digitally so you can access it from wherever you are in the world.
