Your Information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Stonewise Construction ("Stonewise", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

  • Contact form details: your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and the message you send us, including details about your project or location.
  • Email correspondence: if you contact us by email or through a mailto: link, we receive your email address and the content of your message.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log your IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and performance purposes.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes or project information
  • To communicate with you about services you have asked about
  • To improve the website and keep it secure
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

3. Third-party services

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, which receives your submission and forwards it to us. Formspree handles that data under its own privacy policy. Our site is also served by a hosting provider that may process technical data on our behalf. These providers only handle data as needed to run the services we use.

4. Cookies and local storage

This site does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics or other tracking tools in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain a reasonable record of our communications, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

6. Sharing and international clients

We do not share your information with third parties except our service providers, professional advisers, or where required by law. Many of our clients live outside Ghana. If you contact us from abroad, your information will be transferred to and processed in Ghana and by our service providers, which may be located in other countries.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

In line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you may ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information about you and give you a copy
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it
  • Stop using your information for a particular purpose

To make a request, email us at the address below.

9. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
